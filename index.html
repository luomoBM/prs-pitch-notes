Pitch notes

Most soccer games chase realism. This one hangs its athletes from ropes and lets physics write the highlights: a pull-back for power, a swing for the arc, a release for the flight.

What the rope teaches

Pendulum speed peaks at the bottom of the swing — which is where driven shots leave. Big arcs for distance, small arcs for control, and the bounce forgives everything else.

Where to play

Ragdoll Soccer runs free in the browser: ten puzzle levels solo, or a 2 Player match on one keyboard — about the site. By RHM Interactive, nothing to install.
